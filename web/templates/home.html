How it works
  1. 1
    Create or upload. Generate a BeWER report from transcripts on the Create page, or upload one you already have.
  2. 2
    Review & classify. Every reference/generated difference is categorized by type and clinical risk — formatting, replacement candidate, context-dependent, or misrecognition.
  3. 3
    Exclude the noise. Mark formatting/normalization differences and fixable shorthand as excluded so they don't count against the model. Flag anything for follow-up.
  4. 4
    Re-run WER. Recompute WER/CER (and Medical Term Recall) with the excluded items corrected, to see the model's true error rate.
WER best practices
WER measures overall accuracy, but measuring it reliably and accurately is difficult.
MTR compliments WER: the best STT models balance medical and everyday language, and adapt to the formatting demands of the target workflow.
Check your work: quality audio → correct configuration → a gold-standard transcript → verify the alignment.
Tympany supports the “check your work” step — separate real errors from formatting/normalization noise.
Many “bad transcript” reports aren't caused by the STT model itself.
Before concluding a model is wrong, rule out the far more common causes:
  • Audio quality. Mind microphone selection, placement, and calibration; use mono or dual-mono multichannel; capture at 16 kHz, 16-bit; and account for room acoustics.
  • Validate integration. Use the right endpoint for the workflow (dictation/UI control, live conversations, or batch files) and confirm configuration — formatting, punctuation, commands, diarization.
  • Gold transcript. The reference test must represent what is in the audio file and formatted to match what the model is configured to output.
Risk: Misaligned evaluations. A reference that doesn't match the formatting config, or a gold transcript that doesn't match the audio, inflates WER with differences that aren't real errors.
Solution: Tympany surfaces these as formatting/replacement differences so you can exclude them and measure true misrecognitions.
