How it works

Upload a BeWER report and Tympany will classify every reference/generated text difference and classify each one:

Formatting error Low risk Difference between reference and generated text for the written form for numbers, dates, or years.
Replacement candidate Low risk Abbreviation, acronym, or other shorthand in reference that can be handled with a replacement rule defined in STT configuration.
Context-dependent Medium risk Spelling variant, compound boundary, or subtle error that likely does not change the meaning of the STT output but should be reviewed.
Misrecognition High risk STT errors with the largest edit distances that may have clinical significance or otherwise alter the intended meaning.
